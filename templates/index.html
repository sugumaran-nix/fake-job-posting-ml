Fraud intelligence

The patterns that expose a scam — every time.

💸

Registration fee demands

Any request for a "joining fee," "registration kit," or "starter deposit" before employment starts is a near-certain scam. Real employers never charge applicants — not ₹99, not ₹799, not ₹5,000.

Most common pattern — present in 68% of scam postings
🏦

Financial data fishing

Upfront requests for Aadhaar, PAN, bank account details, or OTPs during application. Legitimate employers request documents only after issuing a formal offer letter.

🚨 India's PAN card was the world's most-targeted ID document in 2024 — 27% of all global identity attacks.

⚠️ Once shared, identity data is sold within 48 hours on dark web marketplaces.

✅

False income guarantees

"₹50,000/month guaranteed" or "100% assured placement" with no interview process. No legitimate employer can guarantee income before meeting you.

The threat is evolving

AI is making scam postings indistinguishable from real ones.

Before 2024, scam postings were identifiable — poor grammar, copied text, unrealistic promises. Scammers now use LLMs to generate professional, company-specific, convincing descriptions in seconds.

Grammar is no longer a signal. Industry terminology is no longer a signal. Even a matching company website can be spun up in minutes. Behavioural patterns are what remain.

JobGuard analyses those patterns — the things AI-generated scams cannot hide, regardless of how polished they look.
