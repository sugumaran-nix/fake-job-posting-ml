DATA & CONTROL

Privacy & data

What JobGuard stores when you check a posting, and how to remove it.

At a glance

Use JobGuard without an account.

JobGuard does not ask you to create a profile. When you run a check, the submitted job details and analysis result are saved in History so you can review, filter, export, or remove them later.

What is stored

  • Submitted job fields, including the description, requirements, title, company, location, salary, and website.
  • The verdict, probability scores, model name, URL risk, and submission time.

Your controls

  • Remove a single record from History with its delete action.
  • Use Clear all to remove the saved History records together.
  • Export a CSV summary. The export does not include the full description or requirements text.

Use safer inputs

Submit the job posting you want to check, not your own identity documents or financial information. Do not paste Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, passwords, or private correspondence into the form. JobGuard’s analysis is a screening aid and should be followed by independent verification.
