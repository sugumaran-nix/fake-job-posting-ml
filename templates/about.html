About JobGuard

How it works, what it detects, and why it was built

The problem

India loses billions to fake job scams every year. Fraudulent postings target fresh graduates, job seekers, and rural applicants — offering guaranteed income, WFH roles, and government jobs that don't exist.

They ask for registration fees, Aadhaar numbers, and bank details. Most victims never recover their money. JobGuard was built to give every job seeker a fast, free fraud check before they apply — or pay.

A BERT-base transformer fine-tuned on the EMSCAD dataset — 17,880 real job postings labelled as fraudulent or legitimate. The model reads the full job description as a sequence, capturing long-range context that bag-of-words approaches miss.

On top of BERT, a rule-based layer checks 8 fraud pattern categories, scores the company URL across 10 signals, and flags known suspicious terms. Every prediction includes a per-token influence breakdown so you understand exactly why the model decided what it did.
