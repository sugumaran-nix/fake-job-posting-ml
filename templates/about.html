About JobGuard

System design, dataset, pipeline, and architectural decisions.

What is JobGuard?

JobGuard is an NLP-powered fraud detection system for job postings. It combines a Linear SVM trained on 17,880 real-world records from the EMSCAD dataset with an independent 10-check URL fraud analysis engine. The two signals are fused into a single combined risk score — giving users both a machine learning verdict and an automated domain credibility assessment in under a second.

Built as an MCA final-year project at Anna University, Coimbatore (2025–26). The system targets entry-level job seekers most vulnerable to work-from-home scams, data-harvesting postings, and fee-charging fraud.
