About JobGuard

How it works, what it detects, and why it was built.

Why this exists

Fake job scams cost Indian job seekers thousands of crores every year. Fraudulent postings target fresh graduates, rural applicants, and anyone desperate for income — offering guaranteed salaries, WFH roles, and government placements that don't exist.

They ask for registration fees, Aadhaar numbers, and bank details upfront. Most victims never recover their money. JobGuard gives every job seeker a fast, free fraud check — before they apply, or pay.

JobGuard uses an ensemble of classical ML classifiers (Logistic Regression, Linear SVM, Naive Bayes, Random Forest) trained on the EMSCAD dataset — 17,880 real job postings labelled as fraudulent or legitimate. The best-performing model by fraud-class F1 is selected automatically.

On top of the classifier, a rule-based layer checks 8 fraud pattern categories and scores the company URL across 10 signals. Every prediction includes the top contributing tokens so you understand exactly why the model flagged the posting.
